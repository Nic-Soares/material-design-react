`Tabs` takes `variant="bar"`: the portfolio's Tab Bar, a row of pill tabs sharing a surface-variant bar, with a primary selection pill that slides to the selected tab, in three sizes (`size`: `small`, `medium`, `large`, or `responsive` by window size class). `TabList` takes `sticky` for it: the bar sticks at `--md-tabs-sticky-top` and, once stuck, shrinks to the small size. Same roles and keyboard as the M3 tabs. `FocusRing` takes a `className`.
