Add `Ripple`, the M3 pressed state for interactive surfaces (a port of Material Web's ripple; its Apache-2.0 notice ships in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`), and `useReducedMotion`. The ripple takes its color, opacity and easing from `--md-ripple-pressed-color`, `--md-ripple-pressed-opacity` and `--md-ripple-easing`, which default to the M3 system tokens.
