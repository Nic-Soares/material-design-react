Add `Menu` with `MenuItem` and `MenuDivider`, `LinearProgress` and `Skeleton`. The menu opens from any anchor element in a portal, flips when there's no room, and supports full keyboard navigation and single-choice items (`menuitemradio`). The linear progress indicator follows M3 Expressive (gap and stop dot, 4 or 8px), determinate or indeterminate. The skeleton is a decorative loading placeholder.
