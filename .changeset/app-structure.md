Add `TopAppBar`, `NavigationBar`, `NavigationRail` and `NavigationItem`. The top app bar has leading, title and trailing slots, small or center-aligned, and takes the surface-container color when content scrolls under it. Navigation items are links that mark the current destination with `aria-current="page"`; pass `render` to draw them through any router's link component, since the library imports no router.
