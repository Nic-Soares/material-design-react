Add `StateLayer` (M3 hover and focus states, pure CSS), `FocusRing` (the M3 keyboard focus indicator, with its pulse) and `IconSlot` (sizes an icon from `--md-icon-size` and colors it from the text). With `Ripple`, these are the pieces every interactive component uses; each takes its values from `--md-state-layer-*`, `--md-focus-ring-*` and `--md-icon-size`, defaulting to the M3 system tokens.
