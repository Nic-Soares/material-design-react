New `Fab`: the M3 floating action button in four sizes (`small` 40px, `regular` 56px, `medium` 80px, `large` 96px) and six colors, with the icon as a slot. With `label` it is an extended FAB; with `href` it renders an `<a>`, as `Button` does. It fits a `NavigationRail`'s `header`.
