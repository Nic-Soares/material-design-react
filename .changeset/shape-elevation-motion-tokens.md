Add the shape, elevation and motion tokens. `--md-sys-shape-corner-*` follows the Material 3 Design Kit, including the M3 Expressive *increased* steps; `--md-sys-elevation-level0` to `level5` are the kit's shadows, colored from the scheme's shadow role. Motion follows M3 Expressive's springs: `--md-sys-motion-spring-{fast,default,slow}-{spatial,effects}-easing` and `-duration`, with `data-motion="standard"` for the standard springs, plus the M3 duration and easing tokens.
