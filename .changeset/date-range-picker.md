Add `DateRangePicker`, the M3 date range picker: a filled or outlined field that opens a calendar, docked under it or in a modal dialog. The first day chosen starts the range and the second ends it; the range applies on confirm. The calendar follows the WAI-ARIA date grid (arrows, Home, End, Page Up and Down, with Shift by year), honors `min` and `max`, and takes month names, weekday names, the first day of the week and the range format from the `locale` through `Intl`. With `name`, it submits `<name>-start` and `<name>-end` as ISO dates.
