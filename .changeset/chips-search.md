Add the four M3 chips — `AssistChip`, `FilterChip`, `InputChip` and `SuggestionChip`, outlined or elevated — and `SearchBar`, a native search field with a clear button. Filter chips toggle with `aria-pressed`; input chips have a separate, named remove button; assist and suggestion chips can be links with `href`.
