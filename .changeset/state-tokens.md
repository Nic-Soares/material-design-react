Add the Material Design 3 state layer opacities as `--md-sys-state-*-state-layer-opacity` tokens: hover 0.08, focus 0.1, pressed 0.1, dragged 0.16. Mixed with a color role through `color-mix()`, they give hover, focus, pressed and dragged states that follow any color scheme.
