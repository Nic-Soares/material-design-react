Add the Material Design 3 type scale as `--md-sys-typescale-*` tokens: font, weight, size, line height and tracking for every display, headline, title, body and label style, baseline and emphasized (`--md-sys-typescale-emphasized-*`), in `rem`. Fonts come from `--md-ref-typeface-brand` and `--md-ref-typeface-plain` (Roboto, then system-ui); the library ships no font files.
