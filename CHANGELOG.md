# @nic-soares/material-design-react

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 83fd15d: `Button` renders a link (`<a>`) when given `href`, for navigation; a disabled link loses its `href` and gets `aria-disabled`. New `onRippleEnd` runs once a press's ripple has faded, so an app can navigate after the press is seen. `ButtonProps` is now a union of `ButtonAsButtonProps` and `ButtonAsLinkProps`.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- dff6c17: Add `Card` (elevated, filled or outlined; the whole card becomes a link with `href`), `Divider` (full width, inset or vertical) and `CircularProgress`, the M3 Expressive circular indicator with a gap between indicator and track, determinate or indeterminate.
- 63ef9be: Add `DateRangePicker`, the M3 date range picker: a filled or outlined field that opens a calendar, docked under it or in a modal dialog. The first day chosen starts the range and the second ends it; the range applies on confirm. The calendar follows the WAI-ARIA date grid (arrows, Home, End, Page Up and Down, with Shift by year), honors `min` and `max`, and takes month names, weekday names, the first day of the week and the range format from the `locale` through `Intl`. With `name`, it submits `<name>-start` and `<name>-end` as ISO dates.
- 742a2ec: Add `Dialog` (basic, with an optional icon, or full-screen) and `NavigationDrawer` with `NavigationDrawerItem`, the modal drawer for compact windows. Both are controlled (`open`, `onClose`) and built on the native modal `<dialog>`: focus stays inside and returns on close, the page behind is inert and doesn't scroll, and Escape or a click on the scrim asks to close. Drawer items take `href` or a `render` prop for any router.
- 2820d56: Add `Select`, the M3 select: a filled or outlined field with a floating label, supporting text and error state that opens a menu of `options`. It follows the WAI-ARIA select-only combobox (focus stays on the field; arrows, Home, End, Page keys and letters move; Enter, Space or a click chooses; Escape closes), is controlled or uncontrolled by `value`, and submits its value with a form through `name`.
- 4a3226a: Add `Tabs` with `TabList`, `Tab` and `TabPanel`, primary or secondary, controlled or uncontrolled by `value`, with the WAI-ARIA tabs keyboard pattern and an indicator that slides to the selected tab. Add `Tooltip`, plain or rich (with a `subhead`), shown after a hover delay or on keyboard focus and linked to its child through `aria-describedby`.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- bb9e803: Add `TopAppBar`, `NavigationBar`, `NavigationRail` and `NavigationItem`. The top app bar has leading, title and trailing slots, small or center-aligned, and takes the surface-container color when content scrolls under it. Navigation items are links that mark the current destination with `aria-current="page"`; pass `render` to draw them through any router's link component, since the library imports no router.
- 1623d33: Add `Button`, `IconButton` and `ConnectedButtonGroup`, the M3 Expressive buttons: five variants and five sizes, round or square, toggle mode (`selected` / `defaultSelected` / `onSelectedChange`), icon buttons in three widths, and connected groups of two to five segments with single or multiple selection by `value`. Each renders native elements and forwards their props and `ref`.
- 29ea14f: Add the four M3 chips — `AssistChip`, `FilterChip`, `InputChip` and `SuggestionChip`, outlined or elevated — and `SearchBar`, a native search field with a clear button. Filter chips toggle with `aria-pressed`; input chips have a separate, named remove button; assist and suggestion chips can be links with `href`.
- 3d087b3: Add the Material Design 3 color tokens. `styles.css` now defines every color role as a `--md-sys-color-*` variable, generated with Material Color Utilities exactly as Material Theme Builder generates them (spec 2021, Tonal Spot), from the M3 baseline source color `#6750A4`. Light and dark follow the system setting, or `data-theme="light"` / `"dark"` on any element; standard, medium and high contrast follow `data-contrast` or the system's `prefers-contrast: more`. Requires browsers with `light-dark()`: Chrome and Edge 123, Firefox 120, Safari 17.5 or later.
- bf723fb: Add `Menu` with `MenuItem` and `MenuDivider`, `LinearProgress` and `Skeleton`. The menu opens from any anchor element in a portal, flips when there's no room, and supports full keyboard navigation and single-choice items (`menuitemradio`). The linear progress indicator follows M3 Expressive (gap and stop dot, 4 or 8px), determinate or indeterminate. The skeleton is a decorative loading placeholder.
- 3e01983: Add `Ripple`, the M3 pressed state for interactive surfaces (a port of Material Web's ripple; its Apache-2.0 notice ships in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`), and `useReducedMotion`. The ripple takes its color, opacity and easing from `--md-ripple-pressed-color`, `--md-ripple-pressed-opacity` and `--md-ripple-easing`, which default to the M3 system tokens.
- 9ef87a5: Add the `/color` entry point for dynamic color: `createScheme()`, `applyScheme()` and `schemeCss()` build the `--md-sys-color-*` tokens from any source color with Material Color Utilities, as Material Theme Builder does, with a variant, a contrast level and a `spec` option (`"2021"`, Theme Builder's colors, or `"2025"`, the M3 Expressive colors Android uses). Apps that don't import `/color` don't load it.
- 0671a98: Add the shape, elevation and motion tokens. `--md-sys-shape-corner-*` follows the Material 3 Design Kit, including the M3 Expressive _increased_ steps; `--md-sys-elevation-level0` to `level5` are the kit's shadows, colored from the scheme's shadow role. Motion follows M3 Expressive's springs: `--md-sys-motion-spring-{fast,default,slow}-{spatial,effects}-easing` and `-duration`, with `data-motion="standard"` for the standard springs, plus the M3 duration and easing tokens.
- b596d94: Add `StateLayer` (M3 hover and focus states, pure CSS), `FocusRing` (the M3 keyboard focus indicator, with its pulse) and `IconSlot` (sizes an icon from `--md-icon-size` and colors it from the text). With `Ripple`, these are the pieces every interactive component uses; each takes its values from `--md-state-layer-*`, `--md-focus-ring-*` and `--md-icon-size`, defaulting to the M3 system tokens.
- 7cd5065: Add the Material Design 3 state layer opacities as `--md-sys-state-*-state-layer-opacity` tokens: hover 0.08, focus 0.1, pressed 0.1, dragged 0.16. Mixed with a color role through `color-mix()`, they give hover, focus, pressed and dragged states that follow any color scheme.
- c8796a9: Add the Material Design 3 type scale as `--md-sys-typescale-*` tokens: font, weight, size, line height and tracking for every display, headline, title, body and label style, baseline and emphasized (`--md-sys-typescale-emphasized-*`), with the values of the Material 3 Design Kit, in `rem`. Fonts come from `--md-ref-typeface-brand` and `--md-ref-typeface-plain` (Roboto, then system-ui); the library ships no font files.
